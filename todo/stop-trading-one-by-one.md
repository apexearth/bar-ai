# Stop trading units one by one into a big army (his 11-05)

WRDistilled masses a big army and moves it as one; BARb's fight logic, which ours follows, lets units arrive and
die one at a time, so we never field a mass. Our scorecard agrees: against BARb hard, army value 0.25-0.40 of
theirs while metal produced is 0.86-1.00 and damage efficiency 0.85-0.90.

He wants to see when we are feeding units singly into a larger army, and to stop doing it. He judges this a big
overhaul of the fight logic, deferred until the training foundation is strong.

First the instrument: per unit death, own combat units and value nearby and the enemy's local value, for us,
BARb and WRDistilled (the LIFE records and death lines carry most of it; the WRDistilled games already played
can be read without new runs). Then the behaviour, per docs/army.md.
