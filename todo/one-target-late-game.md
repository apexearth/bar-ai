# The whole shabang on one target, late (his 10-23)

*"In very late game, mass LRPC hitting a target, nukes focusing there too, and AIR focusing attacking that area as
well. AND T3. The whole shabang will break down/capitulate that target. Though it is possible other better targets
may come up. because the guy being targetted will reinforce if we don't kill them fast enough. sometimes switching
targets fully and suddenly will catch someone else off-guard."*

One focus target for LRPC, nukes, air and T3 at once, priced by what breaking it is worth against how fast they
reinforce it; switch when the kill rate falls behind their rebuild or a softer target's price passes it -- the switch
whole and sudden, not a drift. Ties to `team-strategies.md` and `issues/decisiveness-team-push.md`.
