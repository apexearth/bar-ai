# Air swarms a slow push (his 10-23)

Of his Canis River win, one ally slow-pushing under 17 LRPCs: *"the thing for your air to do in that case would be to
swarm his slow push, he would have been killed, but he was under jammer and did have AA units, still... all tightly
packed, could kill it"*.

The strike planner's targets are economy only (nnair.as NaSnapEnemy, gNaEcoP), so an army is never a candidate. As
a price: an enemy army blob is a strike cell worth its metal, raised by how tightly it packs (a bomber's splash and a
swarm's focus land on many units), against the AA inside it -- the same NaEval, the AA crossed included
(apex_aa_dwell).
