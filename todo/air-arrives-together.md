*"you also need to position the aircraft so they reach the target in a reasonable formation and correct timing,
whether its a single wing or multiple"*. A mass that trickles in dies one plane at a time.

What exists: a gunship wing flies in a grid (AssignFlierSlots, `squad air-formation`), a bomber strike takes the
gunships to the same cell, the team air plan gathers seats at one rally. Unmeasured: how far apart they ARRIVE.
First the instrument -- per strike, first to last plane entering the target cell, within a wing and across wings.
Then: gather outside their AA reach, fly a wing at its slowest plane's speed, and launch each wing so all reach the
cell together (launch = arrival time minus that wing's flight time).
