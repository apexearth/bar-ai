# Don't let them mass LRPCs (his 10-23)

Of his Canis River win: *"to defend against my LRPC count of like 17 you'd have needed a ton of shields... best to
just not let me build so many of those"*. Our four seats lost 3.86M metal there, 2.19M to attackers they never saw
(shells from out of sight) and 171k+62k to Berthas and Legion LRPCs by name.

As a price: an enemy LRPC is worth what it will destroy over its life, and one still being built costs little to
kill -- their LRPC frames and first guns are among the best targets for bombers, nukes and raids. The strike
planner offers only economy today (nnair.as gNaEcoP). Needs the look: losses from shells we cannot see point at
where to scout (pain points), and a seen gun stays remembered until seen dead.
