# Walls that funnel and slow vehicles

His 11-06: "walls can do a lot actually but i never see ai do walls well"; "comet catcher is super flat so vehicles
do dominate on it somewhat, tho good use of walls cancels that some"; "using walls to funnel enemies is good, we
can kill em one by one better that way".

What it is for:
- Funnel: a wall line with a deliberate gap makes their army come through one opening, where our towers and army
  meet it a few at a time instead of as a blob (our losses are fights taken outnumbered: 24 2v2s on 11-06 read
  3.9 of ours against 7.0 of theirs near each death).
- Slow vehicles: on flat maps their vehicle raiders reach our outer extractors (1v1 Comet Catcher, 11-06: we lost
  25.5 extractors by minute 16 against their 12.9, mostly to Raiders, Levelers, Instigators and Wolverines).
