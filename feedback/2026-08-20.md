## The late-game air doctrine (2026-08-20) — flak floor and fighter insurance LANDED, spread + scouting partially

Three directives from the 1v1 campaign session, his words:
1. "It is not unusual for air to show up out of nowhere when it was never
   there before. So when you are in the later part of the game you absolutely
   need to have flak spread out around your base." — HeavyAAWant now carries
   an economy floor (1 flak at `apex_flak_floor_income` 60 m/s, +1 per
   `apex_flak_per` 60) independent of sightings. **Placement is not yet
   "spread around" the base** — flak still clusters at the nano block/front;
   a ring/sector placement pass is still owed.
2. "You should also have T2 fighters. If the enemy is cortex and has 10
   dragons you'll definitely die if you don't have a lot of fighters." —
   `apex_air_mandatory_income` lowered 100→60 at all three sites, so the T1
   air plant, the one-minimum T2 air plant, and its income-scaled fighter
   floor arrive in benchmark-1v1 late game rather than after the game ends.
3. "Using some scouts can certainly help you understand what the enemy is
   doing." — ground scouts are fielded (census f3) and air scouts are floored
   on air lines; no change made, but scouting COVERAGE (does the intel
   actually reach the threat readings?) has never been measured.
Also from the same session: "We don't want to pick personas which are very
bad for a 1v1" (AIRBOSS/SILOIST now excluded from duel rolls) and, on the
eco-vs-army budget fork, the weight "is allowed to change throughout the
game" (the income-indexed target curves are that change; deferrals now make
spend follow them).


## COMPLETED — T1-commit for small-map 1v1s (2026-08-20)

"When players do one versus one games, they often don't even reach the tier
two stage... dedicate yourself to a very strong land army and just try to
finish the game in tier one. Obviously, if the enemy starts to attack you
with tier two, you have to upgrade." Implemented as apex_t1_commit: duel +
map area <= 200 map-units, released by a mobile enemy T2-class sighting

army-only spend (max 2 land plants, no air lab) enforced at PlantApproved
plus a nanoframe-reclaim backstop for the C++ plant entrance. Confirmed on
BOTH maps with paired seeds (Altair trade 0.31->0.52; Comet Catcher
0.43->0.76, produced 1.01->1.92, 1W2L3D -> 3W1L2D) and DEFAULT-ON for duels
since 2026-08-20.
